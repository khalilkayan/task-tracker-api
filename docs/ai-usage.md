# AI Usage Rules

## Rule 1 - What I will never paste
I will never paste credentials, secrets, .env contents, real customer data, sensitive personal data, production data, or code I am not authorized to share.

## Rule 2 - What I will always verify before accepting
I will always review the diff, understand what the generated code does, and verify the relevant tests or behavior before accepting it.

## Rule 3 - How I will record AI contributions
I will document important AI contributions in the project notes or commit history and record what the AI produced and what I personally verified.
